Everything your desk phone does, in your pocket

TINIS PBX is the phone system app of S. TINIS CY DIGITAL NET ADVERTISING LTD. Your extension, your colleagues and your messages, wherever you are.

Calls that ring

Make and receive calls on your office extension over Wi-Fi or mobile data. Incoming calls ring like a normal call, even when the app is closed.

Colleagues

See who is available, busy or on a call. Call, video call or message with one tap, and pick up a colleague's ringing call.

Chat

Message colleagues from the same app, with notifications when something arrives.

Voicemail

See your messages, listen to them and call the person back.

Video calls

Video calls between colleagues, from the phone or the computer.

Apple Watch

A ringing call on your wrist with Answer and Decline, plus recent calls, voicemail and favourites.

CarPlay and Siri

Contacts, recent calls and voicemail on the car screen, and "Call ... with TINIS PBX" by voice.

Sign in with a QR code

IT gives you a code: scan it and you are connected. No server settings to type.

How it works

TINIS PBX is the company's own phone system, so there is no sign-up in the app.

IT creates your extension
and gives you a QR code, or a server address, user name and password.
Install the app
on iPhone, Android, Windows or Mac, then scan the code or enter the details.
Call, chat, listen
your extension, colleagues and voicemail are there, in the office and outside it.

On every device you use

One account, the same extension everywhere.

Secure and private

  • The app talks only to the company's own phone system, over encrypted connections. Call audio is encrypted too.
  • Your sign-in is kept in the phone's secure storage (the Keychain on iPhone).
  • No advertising, no tracking and no sale of data. Read the privacy policy.

Emergency calls: TINIS PBX is not a replacement for your mobile phone. It cannot place emergency calls (112 / 999): use the phone's own dialler.
